About Debby

Singer • Songwriter • Independent Music Artist

My Story

Debby Mendoza is a British-Ecuadorian singer-songwriter, worship leader and independent music artist based in London. She writes and performs original music that blends contemporary pop, acoustic, Afrobeats and Latin influences, with songs inspired by love, faith and real-life experiences.

Influenced by contemporary pop and acoustic music, Debby writes songs inspired by faith, love, hope and life's experiences. Every release reflects her desire to encourage others while remaining authentic to her own journey.

Influenced by artists including Alicia Keys, Adele and Whitney Houston, Debby's catalogue spans uplifting pop, emotional ballads, worship music and Afro-pop with reggaeton influences. As an independent artist, she writes, records and releases original music while continuing to build an audience in the UK and internationally through streaming platforms, live performances and social media.

Beyond music, Debby is passionate about using creativity to bring hope, authenticity and encouragement. Whether performing on stage or recording in the studio, her mission is to create music that expresses creativity, leaves a lasting impact and points people towards hope through honest storytelling.
